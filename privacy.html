Your privacy

What Udex does with your data, in plain English.

Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version

  • Udex has no accounts and no server of its own. Your skin type, sessions and settings stay on your phone.
  • To show the UV where you are, Udex sends your location, rounded to about 1 km, to Apple's weather service.
  • RevenueCat helps run subscriptions, and Apple or Google take any payment. We never see your card details.
  • There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Udex is made by Ben Hardy in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the app. In this policy, "we" means Ben Hardy.

For any privacy question or request, email hben18711@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Udex saves these on your phone so that it can work. They are not sent to us, and we can't see them.

  • Your skin type, which Udex works out from your answers during setup. It doesn't keep the answers themselves.
  • Whether timed sessions are on or off. The safety check during setup sets this, and Udex doesn't keep which answer you gave.
  • Your usual sunscreen.
  • Your current session, including your sunscreen, how you're lying and when you turned or moved.
  • A summary of past sessions: when they happened, time in the sun, the highest UV and how much of your limit you used.
  • Your rounded location, place name and latest forecast, which the widget also uses.
  • Your widget design, and whether you have a subscription.

Some of this, such as your skin type, could count as health information, so Udex keeps all of it out of iCloud and Google backups. It won't move to a new phone, so you'd answer the setup questions again and restore your subscription from the paywall. The widget keeps its own copy of your rounded location, place name, latest forecast and design, and that copy can be in a phone backup.

What leaves your phone

Udex shares data with the services below, only for the reasons given. Each must protect it to the same or an equal standard as this policy.

Apple Weather and Apple Maps, for UV forecasts and place search

What it receives
Your location, rounded to about 1 km, and anything you type into place search. Your IP address reaches Apple too, as with any web request.
When
When you use Udex, and about every two hours from the widget, even if the app is closed.
Why
To get the UV forecast, sunrise and sunset, and to find places you search for.
How long it's kept
Apple says it uses this location only to provide forecasts, doesn't link it to you and doesn't track it between requests. It doesn't publish how long it keeps requests.
Who runs it
Apple, which decides for itself how it handles this data. See Apple's privacy policy.

Apple or Google

Location
Your phone's location service finds where you are. Udex then asks it to turn your rounded location into a place name.
Payments
If you subscribe, you pay Apple or Google, under their terms. We never see your name, card details or account.
Their policies
Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

People you choose

If you press Share on a session recap, the image goes wherever you send it. It shows your time in the sun, the highest UV and how many sessions stayed within your limit.

What Udex doesn't do

  • It doesn't show ads or sell your data.
  • It has no analytics or crash reporting tools, beyond the subscription totals RevenueCat shows us.
  • It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • It doesn't track your location in the background.
  • It doesn't use your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Apple Health.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so no server is involved.

Your location

Udex only asks for your location while you're using it. You can say no and choose a place by hand instead.

To withdraw permission later, turn off location for Udex in your phone's settings. The widget doesn't find your position itself. It keeps using the last location saved by the app until you open Udex and choose a new place, or delete the app.

Why we're allowed to use your data

UK data protection law requires a lawful reason for each use:

  • Your location: your consent, given through your phone's permission prompt. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Forecasts and place search: we need these to provide the app you chose to use.
  • Your subscription: we need this to provide what you paid for, and the law requires us to keep sales records.
  • Subscription totals and preventing misuse: our legitimate interest in keeping Udex reliable and sustainable.

You don't have to share your location, but Udex needs a place to show the UV for.

Udex works out your limits and reminders on your phone. This doesn't lead to any decision with a legal or similarly serious effect on you.

Your right to object

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object at any time. Email us and we'll stop, unless we have a strong legal reason to continue.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: kept until you delete Udex, which removes it all from your phone. The widget's copy can stay in an older phone backup until you delete that backup.
  • Apple: see Apple's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: to have your records deleted sooner, email us the order ID from your Apple or Google receipt. We may need to keep some sales records for tax reasons.

Deleting Udex doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, limit how it's used, or get a copy of it. Email us and we'll reply within one month. Because Udex has no accounts, we'll need the order ID from your receipt to find your records. For data held by Apple, contact it directly.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator.

Data sent outside the UK

Apple may handle forecast requests and place searches outside the UK, under its own privacy policy.

RevenueCat stores data in the United States. That transfer is covered by standard contractual clauses with the UK's addendum, under RevenueCat's data processing addendum. Email us for a copy.

Children

Udex isn't aimed at children, and timed sessions are only for over-18s. If you think a child has used Udex and want their data removed, email us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If it's a big change, we'll tell you in the app first.
