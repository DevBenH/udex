Udex support

Help with the UV index widget and sun session timer.

Get in touch

Email hben18711@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. If it's about a subscription, include the order ID from your Apple receipt.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Udex. To change its design, open Udex and go to Settings, then Widget styles.

Open Udex at least once first, so the widget knows where to show the UV for.

Why does the UV differ from another app?

Every UV figure is a forecast, and services use different models. Udex shows Apple Weather's figure, and plans sessions on a more cautious one, because cloud can clear without warning. Altitude and shade also change the real UV where you are.

Change your place

Tap the settings button on Today, then Place, and search for anywhere. Udex stays on that place until you choose Use my location.

Cancel or restore a subscription

To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

To restore Udex Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling, open Udex, go to Settings, tap Udex Pro, then Restore purchase.

Moving to a new phone

Your skin type and sessions stay out of iCloud backups, so they won't move across. You'll answer the setup questions again, and can restore your subscription as above.

A word on safety

Udex estimates your UV dose and is not medical advice. It does not make tanning safe: a tan is a sign of skin damage. Limits reduce the risk of sunburn but can't rule it out, so stop sooner if your skin feels warm or sore or looks pink, and see a doctor about any mole that changes.
